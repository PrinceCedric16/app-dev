# Most Favorite Anime Series
by Prince Cedric M. Perez

When thinking, what anime impacted me the most. I always go back into **Code Geass**.
Although I watch many types of anime, but when what comes to impress me the most for the first time is **Code Geass**.

Here are some basic information about the anime series " **Code Geass** "

### Plot
*Code Geass: Lelouch of the Rebellion* is set in an alternate future where the Holy Britannian Empire has conquered Japan, renaming it Area 11. The story follows Lelouch vi Britannia, an exiled prince who gains a mysterious power called Geass, which allows him to command anyone to obey his orders without question.

Using this power, Lelouch adopts the masked identity Zero and leads a rebellion against Britannia to create a better world for his disabled sister, Nunnally. The series explores themes of power, morality, identity, and sacrifice as Lelouch navigates political intrigue, mecha battles, and personal relationship

### Main Characters
1. **Lelouch vi Britannia / Zero**: Brilliant strategist and anti-hero with the power of Geass.
2. **Suzaku Kururugi**: Lelouch’s childhood friend and moral foil, who fights for change from within the system.
3. **C.C.**: Mysterious immortal who grants Lelouch the Geass and becomes his confidante.
4. **Nunnally vi Britannia**: Lelouch’s kind-hearted sister and emotional anchor.
5. **Kallen Stadtfeld**: Fierce Black Knights pilot loyal to Zero.
6. **Emperor Charles zi Britannia**: Lelouch’s father and the ruler of Britannia, central to the series’ deeper mysteries.

### Strength
- *Complex storytelling*: Intricate plot with political drama, moral ambiguity, and psychological depth.
- *Strategic battles*: Lelouch’s tactical genius makes every confrontation thrilling.
- *Character development*: Rich arcs for both heroes and antagonists.
- *Stylish animation*: Mecha designs and dramatic visuals by studio Sunrise.
- *Memorable twists*: Shocking reveals and emotional climaxes that keep viewers hooked.

### Weaknesses
- Pacing issues: Some episodes feel rushed or overly dramatic.
- Character inconsistencies: A few characters make abrupt decisions that feel unearned.
- Over-the-top moments: The melodrama can be intense, especially in later episodes.
- Geass rules: The mechanics of Geass are sometimes vague or inconsistently applied.

# Verdict
*Code Geass is a must-watch for fans of cerebral anime with emotional stakes. It’s a rare series that balances spectacle with substance, and its legacy continues to influence anime storytelling today. Whether you're drawn to its mecha battles or its philosophical undertones, Code Geass delivers a thrilling ride with a hauntingly beautiful conclusion.*
- Prince (2025)
